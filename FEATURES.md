# Feature list

Ideas to build, found while testing. Newest at the bottom; move an item to "Done" with the commit when it ships.

## To build

1. **Map picker from the title.** Clicking the map's name at the top left opens a menu of every map, sorted in folders (for example Continents, Kalimdor and Eastern Kingdoms zones, Dungeons, Raids, Battlegrounds, Forever's own maps), to jump straight to one instead of stepping through them with `<` `>`; easy to go back and forth between folders and maps. (2026-10-21)
2. **Steep cliffs without streaks.** The 3D ground is coloured from the top-down minimap images, which have no detail on steep faces, so steep cliffs (Orgrimmar's canyon) show stretched streaks; a rock colour or texture for steep faces would help. (2026-10-21)

## Done

1. **Full screen.** A button next to the close button (X), with the art of Blizzard's own maximize / minimize arrow, makes the GPS window fill the screen in every mode (2D, 3D, traditional map), and back to exactly its saved place and size; remembered over a reload; `/gps fullscreen`. (2026-10-21, branch J6)
2. **Map key: GPS or Blizzard's map.** A globe button left of the maximize arrow on Blizzard's Map & Quest Log, and one next to the full-screen button in GPS's windows, choose what the game's map key (M) opens: GPS (in whichever mode it opens) or Blizzard's map, and switch at once. Also on the settings page ("Map key opens") and `/gps mapkey gps|blizzard`. Done with override bindings on the map key's keys, so nothing of Blizzard's is replaced. (2026-10-21, branch J6)
3. **An uneven ground mesh.** Every tile with relief has an uneven mesh precomputed at eleven nested levels (a right-triangle bintree, `Data/Lod_*.lua`, 1.46 MB), dense along cliffs and ridges and sparse on flat ground; the level per tile is chosen as J1 chose the step, and the ground morphs between levels. Far views at the same triangle count and frame time show 6-21 % less height error than J1's even grids (Mount Hyjal 6 %, Stormwind 16 %, Durotar 21 %); zooming, the ground moves at most 6 px in a frame (J1: 14). (2026-10-22, branch J8; Morten's idea)
4. **GPS as the minimap.** *Use GPS as minimap* on GPS's settings page (off by default; `/gps minimap on|off`) puts GPS's tilted 3D view (ground and buildings, *Minimap tilt* 0-45 degrees, 35 by default; its own *Minimap ground detail* and *Minimap building detail*, 1,200 and 1,500 triangles by default, apart from the 3D view's, since 2026-10-22) in the minimap's place, under everything Blizzard's minimap draws itself: your arrow, party members, gathering and tracking blips, quest icons and areas, pings, and the buttons round it. It follows you, turns with you when the game's *Rotate Minimap* is on, zooms with the minimap's own zoom buttons and wheel, is round like Forever's minimap, and keeps the ground in the flat minimap's proportions so the game's blips stay on the spot they mark (within 2 px halfway out, about 6 px near the rim). Done the way Blizzard's own hybrid minimap draws its map (a frame under the minimap, the minimap's ground art switched off), so nothing of Blizzard's is replaced and turning it off puts the minimap back exactly. Blizzard's minimap comes back by itself indoors, in instances, where GPS has no position or terrain, and while the 3D window is open (they share one renderer). Lost: Blizzard's own ground art (and the indoor layout, which comes back indoors), GPS's own quest markers and arrow in the minimap (Blizzard's are shown), and a click on the minimap stays Blizzard's ping (GPS opens with its key). Costs a fraction of the 3D window: nothing while you stand still, a quarter of its Lua time and calls walking (sandbox). (2026-10-22, branch minimap; Morten's idea)
